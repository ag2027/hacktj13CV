Dataset title (from OD Mekong portal):
Baseline Survey Result in Cambodia by villages (mine/ERW risk attributes)

Transformation used to produce training_data.csv (500 rows):
- Kept rows with categorical fields fear_level in {Low, Medium, High}
  and proximity in {Very Near, Near, Far, Very Far}
- Positive class (label=1, unsafe): fear_level in {Medium, High} OR proximity in {Very Near, Near}
- Negative class (label=0, safer/no-mine proxy): fear_level == Low
- Sampled 250 positive + 250 negative rows
- Mapped to rover features:
  distance_cm from proximity bucket + small noise
  turn_hint from fear_level bucket + small noise
- Exported columns: distance_cm,turn_hint,label
